## The evidence stack (the strongest defensible points)

### The problem is large and concentrated where we'd launch
- **1 in 7 Australians (14.3%) change address every year; 35.3% within 5 years.** *(ABS, 2021 Census)*
- **Victoria gained +132,860 people via overseas migration in 2023–24; Melbourne was the fastest-growing capital (+142,600)** — the largest of any Australian city. *(ABS, 2024)*

### The trailing partner is the majority, not a footnote — the killer stat
- **~54% of Australia's Skill-stream visa places go to *secondary* applicants (partners + children); only 45.8% to primary applicants.** *(Home Affairs, Migration Trends 2024–25)*
- → For every skilled migrant who comes *for a job*, there's more than one person who came *for someone else* — the literal trailing partner.

### Isolation is severe — and worst for our hero's risk factors
- **1 in 3 Australians feel lonely** (up from ~1 in 4 pre-COVID); **49% feel isolated.** *(Ending Loneliness Together 2023; Scanlon 2024)*
- **~2 in 3 international students** report loneliness/isolation in their early months; they seek mental-health help far less than locals (**22% vs 57%**). *(Sawir 2008; systematic review 2024)*
- The trailing partner sits at the intersection of **three** documented risk factors at once: recent mover + migrant + (often) visa-dependent woman.

### Why connection = resilience (answers the "from the ground up" prompt)
- **The accompanying partner's failure to adjust is one of the most-cited causes of an entire relocation failing.** *(Sterle et al., Frontiers in Psychology, 2018)*
- **Klinenberg's heatwave "social autopsy": two demographically identical neighbourhoods had a 10× death-rate gap — explained by social infrastructure alone.** The hardest proof that connection is life-or-death resilience.
- **Aldrich: depth of community social ties — not aid volume or disaster severity — is the dominant predictor of who recovers.**
- **Scanlon: sense of belonging collapsed from 77% (2007) to 47% (2024)**, even though 85% still value multiculturalism — falling belonging, intact appetite for connection.
- **Putnam:** newcomers arrive rich in *bonding* ties but starved of *bridging* ties — exactly the gap a common-interest connection tool fills.

### The defensible white space (vs incumbents *and* ChatGPT)
- **Clearest gap: lived, current, hyperlocal *cultural* knowledge** — everyone documents *what to do* (TFN, Medicare); nobody owns a trustworthy home for *how it actually works here* (self-checkout, school-gate etiquette, which GP bulk-bills). Lives only in Facebook groups and caseworkers' heads — ephemeral, unverified, scam-prone.
- **Most underserved user: the trailing partner** — corporate tools serve the employee, settlement services serve humanitarian entrants, Study Melbourne serves students; the accompanying partner is served by *none*.
- **Structurally novel: receiver → contributor** — every incumbent is one-directional; none harvest a newcomer's hard-won knowledge ~6 months later when they're the perfect guide for the next family.
- **The moat: a community living memory that compounds** — Facebook loses it, Reddit stales it, PDFs never had it.
- **Honest caveat:** checklists, visa/logistics, service directories, and generic advice/translation are **already well-solved** (Home Affairs, Ask Izzy, ChatGPT) — integrate them as commodities, don't rebuild them.

### Lived proof from r/melbourne
- Practical pain is real & repeated: **ambulance cover ("$1200 for a 15-min trip")**, myki, toll roads, cold houses, the rental crisis.
- Cultural disorientation is constant: tipping, hook turns, things closing early — *"I arrive here and learn what an eshay is."*
- **The community loves to contribute:** a "moved my family from Sweden" post drew **693 comments** of warm, practical help — including a stranger inviting the newcomer to their birthday. The ground-up engine already exists; it's just ephemeral and unstructured.

### Data reality
- CoM open data = a **real, geo-located demo seed for the inner city** (strong: `cafes` 66k, `free-and-cheap-support-services` with hours/cost/category, public-toilet baby-change flags), but **CBD-only and small**. Full coverage + the defensible cultural layer come from **external + community-contributed** data — which is *on-strategy*: it's what makes the tool **reusable** (swappable data pack) and **ChatGPT-proof** (lived local knowledge).

## Three numbers for the pitch
1. **~54% of skilled-migration places go to the *accompanying* partner/children — the trailing partner is the majority.** (Home Affairs 2024–25)
2. **1 in 3 Australians are lonely; ~2 in 3 international students are isolated in their first months.** (ELT 2023; review 2024)
3. **Connection isn't soft — it's a 10× survival gap** (Klinenberg) and the **#1 cause of relocation success or failure** (Sterle 2018).
